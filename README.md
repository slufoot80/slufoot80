## SafePulse: a check-in app that alerts someone if you don't check in

Set a timer before a showing, a first date, a hike or a night alone. If you don't check in, SafePulse alerts your contacts by SMS, WhatsApp and email with your location. It still alerts them if your phone dies or is switched off.
